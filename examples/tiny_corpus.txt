NeuroMindAI is an experimental language model.
A language model learns patterns from text.
A transformer uses attention to process sequences.
Training adjusts model parameters to predict the next token.
A tokenizer converts text into numbers.
The byte tokenizer maps UTF-8 bytes to integer token identifiers.
A causal attention mask prevents a position from reading future tokens.
Small experiments help validate the training pipeline.
Reliable systems require tests, documentation, and reproducible configuration.
NeuroMindAI is a foundation for careful research and engineering.
